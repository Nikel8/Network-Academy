📚 Packet Journey

1. PC1 checks the destination IP.
2. Because PC2 is in another subnet, PC1 needs a default gateway.
3. The switch keeps VLAN 10 and VLAN 20 separated at Layer 2.
4. A Layer 3 device can route between the VLANs.
5. PC2 receives the packet when Inter-VLAN Routing is enabled.
