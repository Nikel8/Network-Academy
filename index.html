📚 Packet Journey

1. PC1 checks the destination IP.
2. PC1 sees that PC2 is in another subnet and needs a default gateway.
3. The Layer 2 switch keeps VLAN 10 and VLAN 20 separated.
4. A Layer 3 device can route traffic between the VLANs.
5. PC2 receives the packet when Inter-VLAN Routing is enabled.
